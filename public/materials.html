Materials

FDM or resin?

FDM pieces are built from a fine strand of plastic, melted in a heated nozzle and laid down one thin layer at a time. The result is strong and dependable, suited to planters, stands, holders and larger works. Look closely and you'll see delicate layer lines, the quiet signature of how the piece was made.

Resin pieces rise from a bath of liquid resin, cured layer by layer under ultraviolet light. They hold the finest detail and a smooth, almost porcelain surface, ideal for miniatures, ornaments, rings and jewellery dishes. The trade-off is that they're more delicate than FDM.

PLA. Our everyday FDM material, derived from plant-based sources. Rigid and crisp, it holds sharp detail and comes in a wide range of colours. It softens from about 55°C, so keep it from hot cars, dishwashers and strong summer sun.

PETG. A tougher filament with a touch of flex. It resists heat to about 75°C and handles moisture well. We choose it for clips, organisers and pieces made for everyday use.

Standard resin. Our choice for the finest detail and the smoothest finish, best for display pieces. It can chip if dropped, and long exposure to sunlight or outdoor weather can make it yellow or brittle.

Tough (ABS-like) resin. Our favourite. Nearly the detail of standard resin, with far more resilience to knocks. We use it for pieces that are meant to be handled and worn.

Rings. Every resin piece is fully washed and UV-cured before it leaves us. If your skin is sensitive, wear a new ring for short spells at first.

Finish. Each piece is printed to order, then inspected and finished by hand, with support marks removed and edges cleaned. Faint layer lines on filament pieces are a natural mark of the process, not a flaw.

Care

  • Clean with a dry or slightly damp cloth and mild soap. No solvents, no dishwasher.
  • Keep pieces away from heat sources and long direct sunlight.
  • Display resin pieces indoors. Handle thin parts gently.
  • Planters: use the drainage tray. PLA and PETG are fine with water, but not with constant outdoor weather.

Safety

Our prints are not food-safe and are not toys for children under 3 (small parts). Resin pieces are fully washed and UV-cured before shipping.
