Materials

FDM or resin?

FDM prints build up layers of melted plastic filament. They're strong and practical, good for planters, holders, stands and larger pieces. You'll see fine layer lines on the surface.

Resin prints are cured from liquid resin by UV light. They're extremely detailed with smooth surfaces, ideal for miniatures, jewellery dishes and ornaments, but more brittle than FDM.

PLA: our everyday FDM material. Rigid, crisp detail, wide colour range, made from plant-based sources. Softens from about 55°C, so keep it out of hot cars, dishwashers and direct summer sun.

PETG: tougher and slightly flexible, with better heat (about 75°C) and moisture resistance. Good for clips, organisers and practical parts.

Standard resin: the finest detail and a smooth finish. Best for display pieces. Can chip if dropped, and turns yellow or brittle with long UV or outdoor exposure.

Tough (ABS-like) resin: detail close to standard resin, but more impact-resistant. Used for parts that get handled.

Care

  • Clean with a dry or slightly damp cloth and mild soap. No solvents, no dishwasher.
  • Keep pieces away from heat sources and long direct sunlight.
  • Display resin pieces indoors. Handle thin parts gently.
  • Planters: use the drainage tray. PLA and PETG are fine with water, but not with constant outdoor weather.

Safety

Our prints are not food-safe and are not toys for children under 3 (small parts). Resin pieces are fully washed and UV-cured before shipping.
