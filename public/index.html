About

With passion for design, art and craft, every piece is unique. Crafted.i.mentions. specialises in skulls, gothic oddities, and curious pieces — made to leave your shelves a little haunted. Each piece starts as a careful FDM or resin print, then gets a hand finish so paint, wear, and detail land with a real twist — never factory-flat.

How it's made

  1. Design

    Every curve and edge is checked on screen before resin or filament ever touch the plate.

  2. Print

    Made to order, one layer at a time, in FDM or resin.

  3. Finish

    Hand finished so paint, wear, and detail land with a real twist.
