About

Crafted.i.mentions. specialises in physical 3D prints for people who like their shelves a little haunted. Skulls, gothic oddities, and curious pieces start as careful FDM or resin prints, then get a hand finish so paint, wear, and detail land with a real twist — never factory-flat. With passion for a craft, every piece is unique: rings, Halloween chess, scarecrows, pirate chests, skull bowls, wall art, and one-offs that feel made for you, not stamped for a crowd.

How it's made

  1. Design

    The model is shaped around how the object will be held and used.

  2. Print

    Made to order, one layer at a time, in FDM or resin.

  3. Finish

    Hand finished so paint, wear, and detail land with a real twist.
