Privacy

We keep things simple — and we don’t haunt your inbox for sport. What we do keep is what we need to make and send your order: name, email, delivery address, what you bought, and what you paid.

Checkout

Your secrets are safe, and so is your card — we never see it. Payment runs on a secure page we don’t control. Card details stay with the payment provider; we never see or store them.

Sharing

We don’t sell your data. It only goes to the payment provider (to take the money) and the courier (to get the parcel to you).

Messages

If you write to us, we keep your name, email, and message so we can reply — nothing spookier than that.

Who to contact

Crafted.i.mentions. For privacy questions, email us once the shop address is live.
